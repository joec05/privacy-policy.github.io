Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kinolist ("We," "Us," or "Our") handles information when You use the Kinolist mobile application (the "Service"). We are committed to protecting Your privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience.

1. Definitions

  • Service: Refers to the Kinolist mobile application and any related software or features provided by Us.
  • User/You: Refers to any individual who accesses or uses the Service.
  • Anonymous Analytics Data: Non-personally identifiable information collected automatically to understand app usage, crash reports, and performance metrics.

2. Information We Collect

Kinolist does not require user registration or authentication. We do not collect, store, or process personal identifiers such as Your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials.

We only collect unidentified technical and usage data, which includes:

  • Device and App Usage Metrics: Information such as operating system version, device model, screen interactions, and app event logs to improve app functionality and user experience.
  • Performance & Diagnostic Data: Crash reports and performance metrics collected automatically to diagnose technical issues and improve stability.

3. How We Use Information

The anonymous analytics data collected is strictly used for:

  • Monitoring app stability, performance, and feature usage.
  • Identifying and fixing software bugs or crash occurrences.
  • Enhancing overall app design and navigational experience.

4. Data Sharing & Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data. We utilize trusted third-party service providers (such as Firebase Analytics and Sentry) solely to assist in gathering aggregate performance and crash diagnostic statistics. These services collect anonymous device parameters and usage metrics and are contractually obligated to maintain data security and confidentiality.

5. User Accounts & Data Retention

Because Kinolist operates without user accounts or personal registration, We do not retain any personal user records. Any local application preferences or cached media state remain stored locally on Your device.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 16. If You believe that any personal data has been transmitted unintentionally, please contact Us immediately.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If You have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Us at support@kinolists.com.
